## 🧭 Page Structure

1. **Navbar**: Floating glassmorphism navbar (80px height) with abstract shield/arrow/L logo, center links, and "Track Claim" / "Start Claim" buttons.
2. **Hero Section**: Two-column layout with a pill badge, massive bold headline, and a right-side floating Claim Tracker dashboard.
3. **Trust Metrics Section**: 4-column white cards grid with metrics like `₹4.2Cr Claims Processed`.
4. **Services Section**: Centered grid of premium cards for Salary Recovery, Freelancer Payments, airline claims, etc., with rich hover interactions.
5. **How It Works Section**: Dark premium section (`#111827`) with a 4-step timeline, glass cards, and glowing gradient connection lines.
6. **Live Claim Tracker Section**: Premium SaaS-style dashboard mockup with charts, timeline, sidebar, and progress bars.
7. **Testimonials Section**: Carousel style layout of white premium shadow cards (e.g. `Recovered ₹85,000 security deposit in 12 days`).
8. **Pricing Section**: Unified flat pricing (₹999 per case) featuring 3 legal notices and 1 police complaint draft sent week-by-week.
9. **FAQ Section**: Clean accordion-style FAQs with smooth open animations.
10. **Final CTA Section**: Dark premium section with a subtle red glow, centered bold copy, and start buttons.
11. **Footer**: Modern minimal footer with quick links and legal pages.
